As Preschool is a not-for-profit organisation and a registered charity we rely on fundraising and support from our parents as well as the wider community.

The Events Team are a small group of parent volunteers who along with help and support from other parents and staff organise and run a handful of fundraising events throughout the year. Money raised goes towards purchasing special equipment or resources. In recent years it has helped to fund the outside shade / canopy and climbing frame.

Past popular events include:

- Menstonbury Festival
- The Christmas Mooch
- Band Night
- Nearly New Sale
- Halloween and Easter fundraisers

If you would like to get involved or could offer help then we’d love to hear from you. From organising a project, to selling raffle tickets for an hour, it all really helps! Please contact our Events Coordinator at [fundraising@menstonpreschool.org](mailto:fundraising@menstonpreschool.org).
