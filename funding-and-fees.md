## Early years funding policy

### Early education funding

- Menston Preschool offers government funded early years education, Monday to Friday between 9am and 3pm, for a maximum of 38 weeks per year (term time only).
- Early education funding is applied for by Preschool and is paid by Bradford Metropolitan District Council on a termly basis.
- To claim funding, parents must complete and sign the Bradford Council Early Years Funding agreement form. This form, along with full support, will be provided as part of the admissions process.
- Funding can be split between different childcare providers. The details of the other setting or settings will also need to be provided. Funding cannot be transferred from one provider to another mid-term.
- If a child starts attending Preschool part way through a term, after the local authority census date, funding cannot be claimed until the following term.

### Funded places for 3 and 4 year olds

Funded places are available from the start of the term after a child's third birthday.

1. **Universal entitlement:** 15 hours of funded early education per week for all children aged 3 and 4 years.
2. **Working families entitlement:** an additional 15 hours of funding per week for eligible 3 and 4 year olds. This is available to working parents who meet the government criteria. To claim these additional hours, parents need to apply for an eligibility code and keep it up to date. Parents must provide this code on the funding agreement so it can be verified by Preschool before funding is claimed on their behalf. A new place cannot be offered where a code is in its "grace period".

### Funded places for 2 year olds

1. **Working families entitlement:** 30 hours a week of early education funding for eligible 2 year olds. This is available to working parents who meet the government criteria. To claim these hours, parents need to apply for an eligibility code and keep it up to date. Parents must provide this code on the funding agreement so it can be verified by Preschool before funding is claimed on their behalf. A place cannot be offered where a code is in its "grace period".
2. **Terrific Two Year Old entitlement:** 15 hours per week of funded early education for children who meet the government eligibility criteria for families receiving additional support.

## Fees policy

This policy explains our fees and payment terms and conditions. Menston Preschool runs on a three-term calendar. Invoices are issued termly through the Tapestry system, in the second and third week of each term, in the following circumstances:

1. You do not claim government funding for your child's attendance at Preschool. For example, you claim your funded hours with an alternative provider.
2. **Additional hours:** you pay for additional hours on top of any funded hours claimed. For example, if you split your funding with another provider, or your child attends more hours than you are eligible to claim funding for.
3. **Early start session:** funded hours are only offered between 9am and 3pm.
4. **Snack, non-food consumables, resources and enhanced provision charge:** a sessional fee, applicable to government funded hours only. The hourly fee for non-funded attendance already includes this amount.

We do not want this charge to be a barrier to attendance for any child. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the Business Manager, Emily Newitt: [office@menstonpreschool.org](mailto:office@menstonpreschool.org).

### Payment of fees

- Fees can be paid in weekly or monthly instalments by arrangement, by bank transfer or cash. We also accept payments from tax-free childcare accounts and childcare vouchers from all the main providers. Please tell the Business Manager if you intend to pay using one of these methods.
- Fees are reviewed annually.
- Fees will not change mid-term. In exceptional circumstances they may change part way through a school year, to take effect at the start of the following term. Any change will be communicated at least one month in advance.
- If for any reason parents or carers choose to end their child's place at Preschool, they are required to give 4 weeks' notice in writing, during which time fees will be payable. Individual circumstances will always be taken into consideration.
- Fees are not charged for bank holidays when Preschool is closed.

### Fees are non-refundable when

1. Non-attendance is due to parental choice.
2. Non-attendance is due to the child's illness.
3. Holidays are taken during term time.
4. Your child does not settle in.
5. There is a short notice or emergency closure.

### Childcare vouchers and tax-free childcare accounts

Menston Preschool accepts childcare vouchers from all the main providers as payment for all fees. We also accept payments from government tax-free childcare accounts. For more information about how to pay using these methods, please speak to the Business Manager.

### Assistance with Preschool fees

If you require assistance with payment of your Preschool fees, please speak to the Business Manager before your child starts Preschool, or as soon as possible after receiving your invoice.

### Late payment of fees

The Business Manager keeps an up-to-date record of fee payments. If payment has not been received by the declared deadline, a reminder will be issued with a deadline for payment. If payment is still not received by the deadline, the matter will be referred to the Treasurer.

If you think you are going to have problems paying fees within the stated timescales, please contact the Business Manager at the earliest opportunity. They will be willing to discuss alternative arrangements for payment. If fees remain unpaid, the Business Manager or Treasurer will refer the matter to the Board of Trustees.

### Non-payment of fees

Any decision taken by the Trustees will first take into consideration the welfare of your child and the wider interests of the group. However, at this point the Trustees reserve the right to remove the child from the session list. The Trustees also reserve the right not to re-introduce the child's sessions once payment has been received.

You will be asked to attend a meeting with the Chair and Business Manager to discuss the Committee's decision, which will include agreeing a schedule for paying fee arrears. If the repayment schedule is not agreed or maintained, the Trustees will seek advice from other authorities, including legal representatives.
