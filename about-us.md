Menston Preschool is situated in the heart of the village in a fantastic, open plan, purpose-built setting that was previously the home of the Children’s Centre. In 2020 we celebrated our 50th birthday!

We are very fortunate to have great outside facilities in the form of a covered decking area and further large outside space. These areas are an extension of the indoors provision and can be used in all weathers.

Menston Preschool employs a team of enthusiastic, dedicated and highly skilled staff. All of whom are passionate and committed to supporting our children as they take the first exciting steps on their learning journey.

As a registered charity we are led by a committee and board of Trustees. We also rely on the generous support from Parents / Carers as well as organisations and businesses in the wider community.
